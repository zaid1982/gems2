What to include: • Specific work activities (welding, cutting, assembly, etc.)
• Equipment or systems involved

Example: "Hot work welding repair on damaged pipeline flange connection in Processing Unit A, estimated 4 hours duration"
Specify the exact location where work will be performed
Be specific about location: • Building/Unit name and number
• Floor level or area designation
• Equipment tag numbers if applicable
Example: "Building 3, Level 2, Pump Room - Equipment P-301A"
Select the type of work being performed
Choose the primary work type: • Hot Work: Welding, cutting, grinding, brazing
• Cold Work: Mechanical assembly, maintenance
• Electrical: Electrical installations, repairs
• Confined Space: Work inside tanks, vessels
• Height Work: Work above 2 meters
• Lifting: Crane operations, heavy lifting
Start date and time for permit validity
When does work start? • Cannot be in the past
• Should be the actual work start date
• Consider preparation time needed
Tip: Allow time for approvals - permits need 24-48 hours for processing
End date and time for permit validity
When will work be completed? • Must be after "Valid From" date
• Include buffer time for delays
• Maximum validity typically 7 days
Tip: If work extends beyond this date, a new permit will be required
Risk Assessment
Assess the overall risk level of the work activity
Risk Level Guidelines: • Low: Routine maintenance, minimal hazards
• Medium: Standard operations with some risks
• High: Hot work, electrical, height work
• Critical: Confined space, major operations
When in doubt, choose a higher risk level for better safety coverage
Identify all potential hazards associated with the work
Common hazards to consider: • Fire/explosion risks • Electrical hazards
• Chemical exposure • Fall hazards
• Equipment failure • Environmental conditions
Example: "Fire risk from welding sparks, electrical shock from equipment, fall hazard from working at height"
Detail the control measures to mitigate identified risks
Control measures should address each hazard: • Safety equipment and PPE requirements
• Personnel assignments (fire watch, safety observer)
• Equipment setup and monitoring
• Emergency response procedures
Example: "Fire extinguisher within 10m, trained fire watch assigned, welding screens erected, gas monitoring every 30 minutes"

PPE Selection Guidelines:
• Mandatory PPE: Required for all personnel on site - safety helmet, safety shoes, and high visibility vest are minimum requirements
• Task-Specific PPE: Select based on work activities - welding requires face shields, chemical work needs gloves
• Environmental PPE: Choose based on site conditions - respirators for dusty areas, ear protection for noisy environments

Examples by Work Type:
• Electrical work: Safety helmet, safety shoes, insulated gloves, safety vest
• Welding: Safety helmet, safety shoes, welding mask, fire-resistant clothing
• Chemical handling: Safety helmet, chemical-resistant gloves, respirator, safety vest
• Height work: Safety helmet, safety harness, safety shoes, lanyard
