SECTION 1: REQUISITION
Enter the responsible person: • Full legal name
• Person who will oversee the work
• Must be present during work execution
Example: "Ahmad bin Abdullah" or "Sarah Johnson"
On-site supervisor information: • Person responsible for day-to-day work supervision
• Must be qualified and experienced
• Available throughout work duration
Example: "Muhammad Ali - Senior Supervisor"
Organization details: • Full company name if contractor
• Department name if internal
• Include registration details if required
Example: "ABC Engineering Sdn Bhd" or "Maintenance Department"
Be specific about location: • Building/Unit name and number
• Floor level or area designation
• Equipment tag numbers if applicable
Example: "Building 3, Level 2, Pump Room - Equipment P-301A"
Choose applicable work types: • Cold Work: Mechanical assembly, maintenance without heat
• Hot Work: Welding, cutting, grinding, brazing
• Confined Space: Work inside tanks, vessels, enclosed areas
What to include: • Specific work activities
• Equipment or systems involved
• Duration and scope of work
• Any special procedures required
Example: "Welding repair on damaged pipeline flange connection in Processing Unit A, estimated 4 hours duration with pre-heating requirements"
Emergency contact information: • Mobile number preferred
• Must be reachable during work hours
• Include country/area code if needed
Example: "012-345-6789" or "+60123456789"
Identification information: • Staff ID number for internal employees
• NRIC number for contractors
• Passport number for foreign workers
Example: "STF001234" or "123456-12-1234"
Supervisor contact details: • Direct contact number for supervisor
• Must be available during work hours
• For emergency coordination
Example: "012-987-6543"
Additional identification: • Contractor registration number
• Professional certification number
• Company registration number
Example: "PKK-12345-A" or "CE-098765"
Work schedule: • Start date cannot be in the past
• Allow time for approvals
• Consider preparation time needed
Tip: Permits need 24-48 hours for processing
Location level information: • Floor number or level designation
• Height above ground if applicable
• Reference landmarks
Example: "Level 3", "Ground Floor", "Basement B1"
