How the bill is composed

The monthly electricity invoice combines contracted usage, connection load charges, and observed maximum demand. Review the breakdown to spot tariff spikes.

  • Maximum demand is calculated using the peak recorder for the month.
  • KWTBB is applied on the electricity subtotal at the configured percentage.
  • Use the daily trend to identify anomalies before invoices are finalised.
